## The Story

Corporate managers complain "nobody wants to work anymore" while offering poverty wages, no benefits, and impossible conditions. The truth? Nobody wants to work at being a slave. Americans aren't lazy - we're rejecting exploitation. Pay living wages, treat workers as humans, and watch "work ethic" magically reappear.

## Verified Facts

- 4.3 million Americans quit jobs in August 2021 alone
- One job supported a family (1965) → Three jobs barely cover rent (2024)
- 87% of workers worldwide disengaged at work (Gallup)
- Real minimum wage lower than 1968
- Source: Bureau of Labor Statistics, Gallup State of the Workplace

## The Reality Check

"Nobody wants to work" = "Nobody wants poverty wages"
We'll work for dreams, not corporate schemes.
