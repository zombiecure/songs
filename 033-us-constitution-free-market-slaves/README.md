## The Story

They say you're free to choose where to work. But when every employer demands you surrender the same rights, when health insurance chains you to jobs, when one missed paycheck means homelessness - that's not freedom. That's slavery with extra steps. The "free market" created a system where you're free to obey or free to starve.

## Verified Facts

- 40% of Walmart workers on government assistance
- 78% of Americans live paycheck to paycheck
- Productivity up 400% since 1979, wages flat
- Health insurance tied to employment traps workers
- Source: GAO reports, Federal Reserve surveys, BLS data

## The New Chains
