## The Story

Before Black folks were freed, before women could vote, before any human rights were constitutionally protected, corporations got their first constitutional rights. In Fletcher v. Peck (1810), the Supreme Court protected a corrupt land deal involving 35 million acres stolen through bribery. Chief Justice Marshall ruled contracts were sacred - even fraudulent ones. Democracy lost its first battle to corporate power.
