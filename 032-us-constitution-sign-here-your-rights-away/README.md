## The Story

Want a job? Sign away your First Amendment rights. Want to work? Waive your Seventh Amendment right to jury trial. Employment contracts force Americans to surrender constitutional rights just to earn a living. Meanwhile, Citizens United gave corporations unlimited speech rights. You have free speech - if you can afford to be unemployed.

Corporations are "persons" with unlimited free speech. Workers are employees who can be fired for any speech.
