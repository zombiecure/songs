## The Story

Sarah McGinnis was a California farmer who thought the Constitution would protect her from railroad monopolies. Instead, she discovered that corporations had been declared "persons" through a court reporter's fraudulent note - not an actual Supreme Court decision. This lie from 1886 still gives corporations more rights than humans today.

## Verified Facts

- Santa Clara County v. Southern Pacific Railroad (1886) - no actual ruling on corporate personhood
- Court reporter J.C. Bancroft Davis was a former railroad president
- Between 1890-1910: Corporations filed 288 14th Amendment cases vs. 19 by African Americans
- The 14th Amendment was written to protect freed slaves, not corporations
- Source: "Unequal Protection" by Thom Hartmann; Supreme Court records

## The Truth

A court reporter's headnote became the foundation for all corporate constitutional rights. No vote. No amendment. Just a lie that became law.
