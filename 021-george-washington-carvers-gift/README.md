## About This Song
"George Washington Carver's Gift" celebrates a man who developed 300+ peanut products and 118 sweet potato innovations, deliberately refusing to patent them because "they should be available to all peoples." His work created what's now an $81 billion global industry, while he lived on $125/month and donated his life savings to education.

## The Truth
Born into slavery, Carver became one of America's greatest scientists, transforming Southern agriculture by showing farmers how to restore soil destroyed by cotton. His peanut innovations created an industry worth $81 billion today, yet he intentionally gave it all away. Major corporations from Skippy to Jif built empires on his freely shared knowledge while Carver died with just $60,000 - which he donated to continue his research.

## Why This Matters
Carver's story offers a different model - brilliance in service of humanity rather than profit. In an era of patent trolls and price-gouging, his example shows another way is possible. His gravestone reads: "He could have added fortune to fame, but caring for neither, he found happiness and honor in being helpful to the world."
