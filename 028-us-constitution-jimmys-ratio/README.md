## The Story

Jimmy Torres worked the factory line for 37 years, trying to save enough to buy his mama a house. She died in the same apartment they'd always rented. When Jimmy started in '85, his CEO made 21 times his salary. Today it's 351 times. The American Dream didn't fail - it was mathematically eliminated.

## Verified Facts

- CEO-to-worker pay ratio: 21:1 (1965) → 351:1 (2020)
- CEO compensation increased 1,085% vs. workers' 24% (1978-2023)
- 90% of children out-earned parents (1940) → 50% today
- Worker productivity up 400% since 1979, wages flat
- Source: Economic Policy Institute, Federal Reserve Economic Data

## The Math of Inequality

If minimum wage grew like CEO pay since 1978, it would be $44/hour today. Instead it's $7.25.
