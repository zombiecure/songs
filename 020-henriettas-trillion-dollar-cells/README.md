# Song 020: "Henrietta's Trillion Dollar Cells"

## About This Song
"Henrietta's Trillion Dollar Cells" tells how Johns Hopkins took Henrietta Lacks' cancer cells without consent in 1951. Those "HeLa" cells became the foundation of modern medicine - contributing to polio vaccines, COVID vaccines, and appearing in over 100,000 scientific papers - while her family couldn't afford healthcare and lived in poverty for 70 years.

## The Truth
Henrietta Lacks died at 31 from cervical cancer, but her cells achieved immortality - they're still dividing today. Companies like Thermo Fisher Scientific generate $40+ billion annually while selling HeLa cells globally. The family finally received a settlement in 2023 after decades of legal battles, but no amount can compensate for 70 years of exploitation while their mother's cells saved millions.

## Why This Matters
This story epitomizes American medical racism: a Black woman's cells created incalculable scientific value while her descendants couldn't afford to see doctors. It shows how systemic exploitation operates - taking everything, giving nothing back, then fighting compensation for decades. The pattern continues today in medical research and biotech.
