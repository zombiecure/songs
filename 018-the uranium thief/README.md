# Song 018: "The Uranium Thief"

## About This Song
"The Uranium Thief" exposes how 4 million tons of uranium were extracted from Navajo lands between 1944-1986 without warning miners about radiation dangers, despite the government knowing the risks since the 1930s. Cancer rates doubled, over 1,000 abandoned mines still contaminate water supplies, and 30% of Navajo households lack clean water today.

## The Truth
From 1944 to 1986, mining companies extracted 4 million tons of uranium from Navajo lands. The Public Health Service knew about radiation dangers since the 1930s but chose not to warn workers. Victor Archer later admitted, "We did not want to rock the boat." The 1979 Church Rock spill released more radiation than Three Mile Island, yet warnings came days late and only in English.

## Why This Matters
This isn't ancient history - 30% of Navajo households still lack piped water, many forced to drink from contaminated wells. Cancer rates in former mining communities remain elevated. The pattern continues: corporations extract billions in profits while Indigenous communities bear the health costs for generations.
