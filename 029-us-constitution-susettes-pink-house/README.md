## The Story

Susette Kelo, a nurse, saved for seven years to buy a little pink cottage by the Thames River in Connecticut. She restored it with her own hands. In 2005, the Supreme Court ruled 5-4 that the city could take it and give the land to Pfizer for "economic development." Pfizer never built anything. The land sits empty. But now any government can take your home for corporate profit.

## Verified Facts

- Kelo v. City of New London (2005) - eminent domain for private development
- Pfizer left town in 2009, land remains vacant
- Justice O'Connor: "Any property may now be taken for the benefit of another private party"
- Corporations have 4th Amendment protections against searches
- Source: Supreme Court records, Institute for Justice documentation

## The Irony

Corporate property has more constitutional protection than your home. They need warrants. You need luck.
