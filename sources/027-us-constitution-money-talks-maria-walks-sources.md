# Money Talks, Maria Walks - Sources and Documentation

## Summary
This song illustrates how Citizens United v. FEC (2010) destroyed local democracy by allowing unlimited corporate political spending, telling the story of Maria Gonzalez's failed mayoral campaign against a chemical company's $2 million opposition spending.

## The Human Cost

Maria Gonzalez's fictional story represents thousands of real Americans who've watched their communities' democratic decisions overruled by corporate money. From Richmond to Denton, from Lafayette to countless other towns, local democracy has been priced out of reach of ordinary citizens, fulfilling Justice Stevens' warning that Citizens United would lead to corporate domination of American democracy.
