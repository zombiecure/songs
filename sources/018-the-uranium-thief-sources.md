# "The Uranium Thief" - Fact Sources

## 1. Four million tons of uranium extracted from Navajo lands (1944-1986)
- **Source:** EPA Report on Navajo Nation uranium mining
- **Link:** https://www.epa.gov/navajo-nation-uranium-cleanup
- **Additional:** U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission data

## 2. Victor Archer quote: "We did not want to rock the boat"
- **Source:** "The Navajo People and Uranium Mining" by Doug Brugge
- **Document:** Public Health Service internal memos
- **Context:** Despite knowing radiation dangers since 1930s

## 3. Cancer rates doubled in Navajo Nation from 1970s-1990s
- **Source:** Indian Health Service epidemiological studies
- **Journal:** American Journal of Public Health (various years)
- **Specific:** Lung cancer rates among uranium miners

## 4. 1,000+ abandoned uranium mines still contaminating water
- **Source:** EPA Navajo Nation uranium assessment
- **Date:** 2023 update
- **Link:** https://www.epa.gov/navajo-nation-uranium-cleanup/abandoned-mines-cleanup

## 5. 30% of Navajo households lack piped water
- **Source:** Indian Health Service / DIGDEEP report 2019
- **Link:** https://www.digdeep.org/close-the-water-gap
- **Quote:** George McGraw, DIGDEEP CEO

## 6. Church Rock uranium spill - July 16, 1979
- **Source:** EPA Superfund site records
- **Details:** 94 million gallons radioactive waste released
- **Comparison:** Released more radiation than Three Mile Island
- **Link:** https://www.epa.gov/navajo-nation-uranium-cleanup/northeast-church-rock-mine

## 7. Kerr-McGee Corporation mining operations
- **Source:** Department of Energy legacy management
- **Timeline:** Major operator 1952-1987
- **Current:** Anadarko Petroleum inherited liabilities
