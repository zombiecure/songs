# Nobody Wants to Work (At Being Your Slave) - Sources and Documentation

## Summary

This song flips the corporate complaint that "nobody wants to work" by revealing how poverty wages and impossible conditions killed American ambition, showing workers aren't lazy but rejecting slavery.

## The Truth Behind "Nobody Wants to Work"

Workers aren't lazy - they're exhausted. They're not entitled - they're asking for what their grandparents had with a high school education. They're not avoiding work - they're avoiding slavery. The song reveals that "Nobody wants to work" is corporate speak for "Nobody wants to work for wages that guarantee poverty, schedules that destroy families, conditions that break bodies, and treatment that denies humanity." When companies pay living wages and treat workers as humans, they have lines of applicants. The real shortage isn't of workers - it's of employers willing to pay what work is actually worth in the richest nation in history.
