# Free Market Slaves - Sources and Documentation

## Summary
This song reveals how the "free market" creates slavery through economic coercion, showing how health insurance, student debt, and at-will employment trap workers in a system where they're "free" to choose between surrendering rights or starving.

## The New Slavery Mathematics

The "free market" has created a mathematically perfect trap:
- Need job for health insurance
- Need health insurance to live
- Need to accept any terms to keep job
- Student debt ensures compliance
- Can't build savings on poverty wages
- Can't quit without savings
- Perfect circular slavery

Workers are "free" in the same way a person jumping from a burning building is "free" to choose between jumping or burning. The song exposes how corporate America discovered it's more profitable to rent humans by the hour than to own them outright, creating slavery through economic coercion while maintaining the fiction of "freedom" and "choice." The constitutional rights to life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness have been reduced to work, obey, and take what you get.
