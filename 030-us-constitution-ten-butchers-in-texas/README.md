## The Story

In 2000, ten butchers at a Jacksonville, Texas Walmart voted 7-3 to unionize - the first successful union vote at any Walmart. Two weeks later, Walmart eliminated all meat-cutting operations in 180 stores nationwide. They'd rather destroy an entire department than accept one unionized store. Those ten butchers learned the Constitution's "freedom of assembly" doesn't apply at work.

## Verified Facts

- Jacksonville, Texas Walmart butchers unionized February 2000
- Walmart eliminated all in-store meat cutting within 2 weeks
- Now uses Lockheed Martin intelligence tools to monitor workers
- 40% of Walmart workers qualify for government assistance
- Source: UFCW records, Wall Street Journal, Congressional testimony

## The Lesson

Constitutional rights to assembly and organization stop at the workplace door. Corporate rights don't.
