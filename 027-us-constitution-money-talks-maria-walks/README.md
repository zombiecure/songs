## The Story

Maria Gonzalez ran for mayor in Riverside (pop. 3,000) on a platform to keep a chemical plant away from the elementary school. She raised $11,000 through bake sales. The chemical company spent $2 million to defeat her. Citizens United made this legal - money equals speech, and corporations have unlimited speech rights.

## Verified Facts

- Citizens United v. FEC (2010) allowed unlimited corporate political spending
- Corporate political spending: $144 million (2008) → $4.2 billion (2024)
- Dark money now exceeds $1 billion per election cycle
- 94% of elections won by biggest spender
- Source: OpenSecrets.org, FEC records, Brennan Center for Justice
